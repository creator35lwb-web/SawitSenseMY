# Reasoning: D23, a negotiable price

**Agent:** SS (Claude Code) · **Date:** 23 Oct 2026

## The question
Should SawitSense show readers a negotiable price that leaves the dealer a margin, alongside MPOB's mill-gate price?

## What can be checked
- MPOB's daily price per 1% OER, by region, at the mill gate (already shown, ADR-002).
- The reader's own dealer offer and the OER on their receipt (typed in the calculator).
- From these: the mill-gate value of the reader's fruit, the gap in RM and %, and the RM value of each step of OER.

Worked example from a reader's receipt, using MPOB's 22 Oct prices: 17.25% OER at RM 750 a tonne.
- North: 47.82 × 17.25 = RM 824.90, so the offer is RM 74.90 (9.1%) below the mill-gate price.
- South: 48.20 × 17.25 = RM 831.45, so RM 81.45 (9.8%) below.
- Today's calculator already rates both AMBER (5–15% below).
- Each 0.25% of OER is worth about RM 12 a tonne at North's price, so a grading dispute of 0.5% is worth about RM 24.

## What can't be checked
- **A dealer's margin.** No published figure was found. MPOB's journal articles on dealers (OPIEJ 2001, "MPOB Daily FFB Reference Price: Towards a More Transparent Market"; OPIEJ 2009, "Management of the Malaysian Oil Palm Supply Chain: The Role of FFB Dealers") give none in their abstracts, and nothing found says MPOB regulates it.
- The margins in the AI chat (RM 45–70 and RM 35–55 a tonne, "most dealers offer RM 750–820") cite no source. The chat also priced a 22 Oct receipt with 23 Oct prices.

## Why not a fixed RM margin
- It would be the first figure in the app without a source, which is what K25 corrected.
- Labelled "fair", it can become a ceiling: a dealer can show the reader "SawitSense says RM 760 is fair".
- Real margins depend on distance, volume and grading, which a regional constant can't capture.

## Recommendation
Show the checkable numbers, and present the existing 5% band as an RM target ("RM 784 or more is within 5% of the mill-gate price") labelled as SawitSense's guide, not MPOB's. Keep the thresholds as Alton's call. Crowd-sourced dealer prices would be real data but need privacy and anti-manipulation work (Module 5), so they stay deferred.
