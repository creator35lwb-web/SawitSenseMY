## Decision 1: Exactly how to run D13

- **Approved:** "every 2 hours, every day".
- **Chosen:** `13 */2 * * *`. The odd minute avoids GitHub's busiest moments at the top and half of the hour.
- **Checked before changing the schedule:**
  - `history.json` keeps one price per CPO date, so 12 runs a day don't shrink the History window.
  - The deploy is dispatched after each data commit, and the `pages` concurrency group cancels superseded deploys, so bursts are safe.

## Decision 2: The watchdog had to change with it

- **Problem:** the data-age alert was 4 days, sized so a long weekend without weekday-only scrapes wasn't an alarm. With daily scrapes, 4 days would hide three days of broken pipeline.
- **Options for the new limit:**
  - (a) 12 hours.
  - (b) 1 day.
- **Chosen: (b).**
- **Rejected:** (a). GitHub has started single runs up to 11.8 h late, so 12 h would raise false alarms.
- **Why 1 day:** it equals about 12 missed runs, which delays alone don't explain.
- **Also:**
  - The watchdog now runs every 6 hours, every day.
  - The alert gives the age in hours: "1 days ago" read badly.
