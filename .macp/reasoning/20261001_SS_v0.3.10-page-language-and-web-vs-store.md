## Decision 1: How to fix the page language (K19)

- **Found:** after #19 merged, the live check showed `<html lang>` as `en-US` once the app started, for every phone.
- **Cause, read from the compiled bundle:** Flutter's `Localizations` widget pushes the app's resolved locale to the engine, and the engine writes it to `<html lang>`. `MaterialApp` declared no locale, so it resolved `en-US` and overwrote the value #19 set at start.
- **Options:**
  - (a) Re-apply our value after Flutter's first frame.
  - (b) Add `flutter_localizations` and declare the locales properly.
  - (c) Declare the reader's locale and the supported locales on `MaterialApp`, with fallback delegates that keep Flutter's built-in labels in English.
- **Chosen: (c).**
  - Flutter then sets `lang` itself, at start and on every change, so the #19 JavaScript could be removed.
  - Flutter's base widgets already support every locale; only the Material and Cupertino labels needed a fallback.
- **Rejected:**
  - (a) is a race against the framework and would break the next time Flutter re-resolved the locale.
  - (b) changes `pubspec.lock`, which can't be regenerated without local Flutter (the D9 constraint).
- **Verified on the preview build before merge:**
  - `lang` is `en`, `ms` or `zh-Hans` for the three phones after start, and switches with the menu;
  - the screens look unchanged;
  - the D11 phone check still passes.

## Decision 2: Web vs store

- **Trigger:** Alton asked for the pros and cons of staying a web app, and suggested a free Google Form with a rating.
- **Considered:**
  - **The web app:** no cost, no gatekeeper, every phone (iPhone too), sharing by link, updates in minutes. On the other hand it's less official-looking, not searchable in the store, heavy on a first visit (15 s on slow mobile), not offline, and gives no built-in usage numbers.
  - **The store app:** credibility, discoverability, ratings and usage numbers without tracking, offline use, and notifications later. On the other hand it's Android only, needs a US$25 account, the 12-tester, 14-day closed test, a second product to maintain, slower releases, and yearly Android updates.
- **Recommended:** web first.
  - The feedback loop can start today with the form (D14) and Discussions (D16).
  - Freshness (D13) matters more to trust right now than the store does.
  - The store's 12 testers are easiest to find through the feedback loop itself.
- **Not decided by SS:** D15 stays Alton's call.
