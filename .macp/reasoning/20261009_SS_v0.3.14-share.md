## Decision 1: How to share from a Flutter web app with no new package

- **Chosen:**
  - the Web Share API through `dart:js_interop` (conditional import, with a stub for tests), shown only on touch screens, as in MoatPillar;
  - otherwise, a panel of plain share links.
- **Rejected:** `share_plus`, because a new package means regenerating the lockfile (the D9 constraint).

## Decision 2: What the link carries

- Only the site address and a pitch in the reader's language: no tracking parameters, nothing about the reader.
