## Decision 1: Which Chinese, and which words

- **Trigger:** D10, which Alton put next. The readers are Malaysian Chinese smallholders.
- **Options:**
  - (a) Traditional characters.
  - (b) Simplified, with mainland commodity terms: 棕榈油, 毛棕油, 鲜果束.
  - (c) Simplified, with the terms Malaysian Chinese-language news uses: 棕油, 原棕油, 鲜果串, 出油率, 收购商, and 北马 / 中马 / 南马 / 东海岸 for the regions.
- **Chosen: (c).** Malaysian Chinese schools teach Simplified characters. Mainland terms would read as foreign: Malaysians say 棕油, not 棕榈油.
- **Also chosen:** "Indicative" is 估算 (estimated), not the literal 指示性.
  - 指示性 is finance jargon.
  - 估算 tells a smallholder plainly that the price is worked out, not official, which is the point of the ethical framework's Path C exception.
- **For the native reviewer to confirm:**
  - 估算 for "indicative";
  - 评定出油率 for "graded OER";
  - 收购商 for "dealer";
  - 实收价格 for "price paid";
  - 绿灯 / 黄灯 / 红灯 for the verdict badge.
- **Confidence:** medium-high on the terms, medium on tone. That is why a native reader reviews before merge.

## Decision 2: A menu, not a toggle

- **Options:**
  - (a) An inline three-way toggle, "EN | BM | 中文".
  - (b) A toggle that cycles on each tap.
  - (c) A menu that lists each language in its own script.
- **Chosen: (c).**
- **Rejected:**
  - (a) takes about 40 px more of the top bar and squeezes the page title at 360 px.
  - (b) A reader who can't read the current language can't predict what a tap will do.
- **Why (c) works:** a Chinese reader finds 简体中文 even when the app is showing English. The tooltip names the button in all three languages.

## Decision 3: Fonts

- **Options:**
  - (a) Bundle Noto Sans SC: megabytes, even as a subset.
  - (b) Use `google_fonts`' Noto Sans SC as a fallback: it downloads the whole font file.
  - (c) Rely on Flutter web's built-in fallback: it fetches only the slices of Noto Sans SC that the page's characters need.
- **Chosen: (c).**
- **Measured on the CI preview build:**
  - Switching to Chinese loaded 13 Noto Sans SC slices, about 400 KB in total, once. The browser caches them.
  - Only Noto Sans SC slices were fetched, not the Japanese face, even with the browser set to en-US. The Simplified forms render correctly in the screenshots.
- **Consequence for D9 (offline-first):** Chinese needs the network the first time it is shown. If D9 goes ahead, the service worker must cache these font slices too. This is recorded in the D9 row of `PROJECT_STATUS.md`.

## Decision 4: What else to translate

- **Found while reading every widget:** five strings stayed English in BM too:
  - the feedback dialog's "Close";
  - the calculator's "Required" and "Invalid number";
  - the verdict badge's GREEN / AMBER / RED.
- **Chosen:** move them into the string tables in all three languages, with the English wording unchanged. It is the same kind of fix, in the same files.
- **Guard:** a widget test fails if the Chinese dashboard or calculator shows any English UI string or a raw key.
- **Not translated:** data labels from the backend, e.g. the region cards' "Derived (MPOC CPO × MPOB OER)" and the MPOC page title. They name the sources as the sources name themselves.

## Decision 5: Language memory is Alton's call (D11)

- Every visit starts in English, so a Chinese reader picks 中文 each time.
- Opening in the phone's language, and remembering the last choice, would fix that. But both change what every visitor sees first. SS raised it as D11 with a recommendation instead of building it.
