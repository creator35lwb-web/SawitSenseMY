## Context

Alton asked for a Share feature, so more people discover the site and give feedback. It is modelled on MoatPillar's share (`docs/app.js`), but without Telegram: the live site only.

**#27 (v0.3.14)** adds a Share button beside Feedback on the dashboard:

- **On a touch screen whose browser has a share sheet:** the Web Share API, so WhatsApp and the reader's own apps.
- **Elsewhere:** a panel with Copy link, WhatsApp, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and Email.
- **What gets shared:** the plain site address (no tracking), with a short pitch in EN, BM or 中文.
- **`index.html`:** `og:image` size and alt text, `og:site_name`, `twitter:card`.
- **Tests:** the link formats, no Telegram, phone sheet versus panel, copy link, WhatsApp, and the panel in Chinese.
- **Screenshots** on the preview build at 360 px, in EN and 中文.
