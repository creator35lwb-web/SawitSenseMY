## Context

Alton merged #15 (v0.3.6, Chinese) and approved D11: "Go as recommendation". SS verified v0.3.6 on the live site; the evidence is in the v0.3.6 handoff.

**The problem.** Every visit started in English, so a BM or Chinese reader had to find the language menu each time.

**#16 (v0.3.7)** opens the app in the reader's language:

1. their last choice on that phone;
2. otherwise the first of the phone's languages the app speaks (any Chinese, Traditional included, gets Simplified);
3. otherwise English.

A choice from the menu is kept in the browser's localStorage as `en`, `ms` or `zh` under `sawitsense.language`. That is not personal data, and it never leaves the phone. Where storage is blocked, nothing is remembered and the phone's language is used. There is no new package: `dart:js_interop` is conditionally imported, with a no-op stub for tests.

**Also in #16.** While testing at 360 px, SS found the dashboard title cut off in English ("Daily Price Dash…") and BM ("Papan Pemuka …"). With BM phones now opening in BM, more readers would see it. Long top-bar titles now shrink to fit, with no wording changes.

**Checked on the preview build**, using phones emulated with different languages, each in fresh storage:

| Phone | Live v0.3.6 | #16 |
|---|---|---|
| Chinese (zh-CN) | EN | **中文**. After choosing English and reloading: EN, with `en` stored |
| Malay (ms-MY) | EN | **BM** |
| English (en-US) | EN | EN |
| French (fr-FR) | EN | EN |

## Decisions Made

| Decision | Rationale | Approved By |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Open in the phone's language, and remember the menu choice | D11 recommendation | Alton ("Go as recommendation") |
| Save only explicit menu choices, not the detected language | A reader who never chooses keeps following the phone. Nothing is stored unless the reader acts | SS (delegated) |
| `dart:js_interop` with a conditional import, not `shared_preferences` | No new package means no lockfile regeneration without local Flutter (the D9 constraint) | SS (delegated) |
| Any Chinese phone (Traditional included) gets Simplified | Readable to them, and closer than English | SS (delegated) |
| Titles shrink to fit rather than being reworded | A layout fix needs no native review; the same wording stays | SS (delegated) |
